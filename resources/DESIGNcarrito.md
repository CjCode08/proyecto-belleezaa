## Brand & Style

This design system drives a fast-moving, high-conversion women’s retail catalog. The aesthetic strikes a deliberate harmony between warm minimalism, contemporary editorial chic, and soft feminine energy. It avoids high-friction clutter in favor of an airy, luminous canvas that frames lookbooks, product drops, and flash capsules with effortless grace.

Key brand pillars:
- **Luminous Warmth**: Clean, breathable cream foundations that elevate apparel photography without stark, sterile tech-like clinical coldness.
- **Dynamic Energy**: High-turnover immediacy delivered through striking berry accents, refined promotional micro-tags, and snappy, fluid tactile interactions.
- **Editorial Softness**: Gentle curved surfaces, refined typography, and whisper-quiet outlines that provide structural clarity while remaining tender and approachable.

## Colors

The palette centers on intoxicating, vivid red-berry notes balanced across an organic, soft-tinted foundation:

- **Primary (`#D82C4E`)**: Vivid cherry red utilized for high-priority calls-to-action, primary add-to-bag triggers, and immediate conversion elements.
- **Secondary (`#FF758F`)**: Blush coral applied for hover surfaces, accent borders, sub-badges, wishlist highlights, and subtle celebratory state fills.
- **Tertiary (`#C01C3C`)**: Velvet rouge reserved for urgency moments, flash-sale identifiers, deep promotional callouts, and high-contrast text links on tinted fills.
- **Neutral (`#1F1B1D`)**: Soft soot/charcoal delivering accessible contrast for body copy and headings without the clinical harshness of `#000000`.
- **Canvas & Surface Fills**: Base page backgrounds reside on `#FAF7F5` (warm off-white cream), layered against pure `#FFFFFF` for primary cards and `#FFF0F3` (soft rose petal) for soft interactive modules, pill chips, and banner backs.

## Typography

Typography blends geometric clarity with an upbeat editorial touch:
- **Headings & Editorial Leads (`Outfit`)**: Modern, rounded, architectural letterforms create commanding yet soft titles for campaign drops, collection banners, and price highlights.
- **Body & Controls (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Highly legible, humanistic sans-serif providing crystal-clear readability for product descriptions, size guides, filter facets, and checkout flows.
- **Badges & Meta Labels (`badge-caps`)**: Uppercase micro-type with tracked letter spacing for product status indicators (`NEW IN`, `LIMITED RUN`, `30% OFF`).

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid grid architecture built for rapid catalog skimming and responsive visual merchandising:
- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column responsive layout with `gutter-lg` (2rem) and `margin-lg` (3.5rem). Product feeds default to 4 columns or 3 columns with an expanded filter rail.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with `gutter-md` (1.5rem) and `margin-md` (2rem), arranging product tiles in 2 or 3 columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `gutter` (1rem) and `margin` (1rem). Product grids collapse cleanly to a 2-column browse card arrangement with sticky bottom filter access.
- **Vertical Cadence**: Generous spacing intervals (`space-xl` and above) isolate campaigns and lookbook stories to sustain an unhurried, boutique feeling amid high inventory rotation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy employs translucent layers, warm surface shifts, and featherweight ambient shadows:
- **Surface Elevation Tiers**: Canvas background (`#FAF7F5`) sits at level 0. Resting cards and module blocks sit at level 1 (`#FFFFFF`) with a delicate hairline border (`rgba(31, 27, 29, 0.05)`).
- **Ambient Shadows**: Elevated drawers, quick-add flyouts, and sticky navigation use warm-tinted diffusion: `0 8px 30px rgba(192, 28, 60, 0.06), 0 2px 8px rgba(31, 27, 29, 0.04)`.
- **Active Card Hovers**: Product cards gently elevate on pointer hover via a subtle transformation (`translateY(-3px)`) and a blooming shadow: `0 12px 28px rgba(216, 44, 78, 0.1)`.
- **Frosted Elements**: Sticky category navigators and mobile bottom navigation use backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) across an 88% opaque `#FAF7F5` base.

## Shapes

The interface embraces organic curvature without slipping into novelty:
- **Base Geometry (`roundedness: 2`)**: Interactive fields, filter tags, and base product cards carry standard `0.5rem` (8px) corners.
- **Feature Cards & Modals (`rounded-lg`)**: Larger display surfaces, lookbook banners, and flyout sheets use `1rem` (16px) corners to feel sculpted and soft.
- **Interactive Badges & Chips (`rounded-xl` to Full Pill)**: Filter buttons, status badges, and swatch selectors adopt a fully rounded pill geometry (`9999px`) for a tactile, friendly aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid cherry `#D82C4E` with crisp white text, subtle pill curvature, and an inner glow on click. Hover switches to `#C01C3C` with smooth 200ms ease.
- **Secondary / Quick Add**: Surface fill `#FFF0F3` with `#D82C4E` label. Transforms to outline-active on press.
- **Icon Buttons (Wishlist)**: Circular frosted-white discs positioned top-right on product images; heart icon animates with a spring-bounce scale (`scale(1.2)`) and fills with `#D82C4E` when favorited.

### Product Cards
- Aspect ratio of image container locked at 3:4 for vertical fashion framing.
- Built-in secondary hover image cross-fade.
- Floating top-left tags (`NEW`, `SALE -20%`, `RESTOCK`) framed in pill badges using `#FFF0F3` background with `#C01C3C` copy, or inverted cherry for flash sales.
- Direct inline color swatch bubbles beneath title; clicking switches the displayed image asset instantly.

### Filter Chips & Facets
- Pill-shaped tags with `0.5rem` horizontal padding and border `1px solid rgba(31, 27, 29, 0.1)`.
- Unselected: `#FAF7F5` surface with charcoal text.
- Selected: `#FFF0F3` base with `#D82C4E` border and matching crimson label with an enclosed dismissal `✕` icon.

### Form Inputs & Size Selectors
- **Input Fields**: Flat white background, `0.5rem` radius, light neutral border. Focus brings a 2px ring in `#FF758F` with 0 outline offset.
- **Size Selector Tiles**: Minimal square-rounded pills; out-of-stock items display a fine diagonal strike-through with lowered opacity (40%).

### Mini-Cart Flyout & Bottom Sheet
- Slide-over sheet with clean divider rules (`#FAF7F5`), showing progress toward free shipping via a crimson filled tier bar.
- Direct increment/decrement counters with rounded tactile ends.
